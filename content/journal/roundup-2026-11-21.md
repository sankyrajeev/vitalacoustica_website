## QSC adds CS15 and CS18 powered subwoofers

QSC has extended its C Class range with the CS15 and CS18, self-powered subwoofers with 15-inch and 18-inch drivers and 2,600 W peak Class D amplification, rated at 133 dB and 134 dB maximum SPL. A selectable DEEP mode extends response to 39 Hz and 35 Hz respectively, and a "Quick Cardioid" preset helps set up several subs to reduce energy behind them. Both can be configured from the rear panel or the QSC Loudspeaker Control app, and are shipping now for DJs, bands, small events, worship spaces and smaller installations.

[Read more at ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/qsc-launches-new-cs15-cs18-self-powered-subwoofers/)

## Geese tour with DiGiCo Quantum225 at both ends of the snake

New York band Geese are touring with DiGiCo Quantum225 DS Pulse consoles at front of house and monitors, linked by an analogue split, with SD-Racks from Worley Sound. Monitor engineer Talia Krause uses the dual-screen layout and onboard processing for 32 inputs, while FOH engineer Ross Harris says the console has cut the outboard he carries on fly dates and has coped with "intense heat, downpouring rain". On some festival fly dates the crew used a standard Quantum225 instead of the DS Pulse.

[Read more at ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/digico-helps-make-sure-geese-delivers-on-fly-dates-on-the-getting-killed-world-tour/)

## Topview launches a hybrid live-action and AI film platform

AI video company Topview has introduced Film Studio, which it describes as the "world's first" hybrid film production platform, combining live-action performance with AI-generated environments, shots and effects. Tools include video relighting with adjustable light angle, colour temperature and softness, new camera angles generated from existing footage, AI-added extras, time-of-day and weather changes, and automatic green-screen replacement with lighting and colour matching. Its chief executive says fully AI-generated films have met resistance from Hollywood, and pitches the hybrid approach as a route to mainstream distribution.

[Read more at TV Tech](https://www.tvtechnology.com/production/scripted-production/worlds-first-hybrid-film-production-platform-debuts)

## LED Global Studio launches with former LED Studio leadership

LED Global Studio, a new UK-based LED display company headquartered in Basingstoke, has launched with former LED Studio chief executive Rob Bint leading product design, engineering and manufacturing. Backed by the BrightWorks investment group, it plans bespoke displays engineered in the UK and built at partner factories in China under its own supervision, with warranties of up to five years. Showrooms are opening in the UK and US, and Bint says a new generation of products is due in early 2027.
