## Diversified joins the Disguise Channel Partner Program

Integrator Diversified has formally joined Disguise's Channel Partner Program, letting it specify and deliver the full range of Disguise hardware, software and immersive services. The deal brings closer access to Disguise's product roadmap, dedicated technical resources and priority support. It formalises work the two have already done together on LED and projection content at Crossroads Church in Ohio, Venu and the Denver Broncos.

[Read more at AV Magazine](https://www.avinteractive.com/news/business/diversified-formalises-its-partnership-with-disguise-24-11-2026/)

## SiliconCore's Enlighten FLEX bends both ways

SiliconCore has launched Enlighten FLEX, a curved chip-on-board LED display that can be installed concave or convex, in 0.9 mm and 1.2 mm pixel pitches. It is rated at 1,000 nits and 18 W per module, with the company's common-cathode design claimed to use up to 20% less energy than comparable bendable products, and a quoted LED lifespan of more than 100,000 hours. Processing runs at 120 fps with 16- to 22-bit depth, and the company's ZACH technology lifts low-level brightness to improve detail in dark content.

[Read more at AV Magazine](https://www.avinteractive.com/news/products/siliconcore-adds-led-for-convex-and-concave-installations-24-11-2026/)

## Mo-Sys opens its Xtend Stage in London with TEDx

Mo-Sys has opened the Xtend Stage, a London presentation space for up to 120 guests, with an event by TEDx University of Greenwich. Speakers presented inside virtual sets, using motion capture to interact with virtual objects in real time, captured by a moving multi-camera production with LED walls, set extensions and AR, and streamed to remote viewers. Mo-Sys says this kind of technology has until now "been reserved for film-makers and broadcasters", and is pitching it at corporate town halls, investor presentations and product launches.

[Read more at AV Magazine](https://www.avinteractive.com/territories-news/uki/tedx-university-of-greenwich-opens-mo-syss-xtend-stage-24-11-2026/)

## iZotope releases Nectar 5 with lyric-based editing

iZotope has released Nectar 5, a vocal production suite of 18 plug-ins. A new ARA plug-in, Moments, transcribes vocals into lyrics so reverb, doubling or formant shifts can be applied to chosen words and phrases, and new tools include a three-stage serial compressor and an adaptive Vocal Stabilizer EQ. The Vocal Assistant can now match a supplied acapella reference, and the suite comes in full and Elements versions.
