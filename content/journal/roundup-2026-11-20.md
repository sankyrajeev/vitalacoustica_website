## Two L-Acoustics systems for Wiesbaden's listed Kurhaus

AVEO has won a public tender to replace the PA in the Kurhaus Wiesbaden, whose 1,350-seat Friedrich-von-Thiersch-Saal dates from 1907 and is under heritage protection. A permanent system of A10i arrays, KS21i subwoofers and A10i, X8i and 5XT fills sits hidden in existing fabric-covered recesses, while a mobile Kiva II system with SB15m and KS21 subwoofers works within a 350 kg rigging limit. Both run from five LA7.16i amplified controllers, with Soundvision used to deal with marble surfaces, reverberation and the shadows cast by columns and balconies.

## A 1,200-drone proposal over Kuala Lumpur

A fleet of 1,200 drones flew around the Petronas Towers in Kuala Lumpur last week, spelling out "will you marry me?" inside a heart and animating a ring being placed on a finger. The show accompanied a proposal by singer and celebrity chef Datuk Fazley Yaakob during the Live 11.11 Mega Shock concert at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre.

## LD Systems covers Porsche Vibe Seoul zone by zone

Porsche Vibe Seoul 2026 drew 15,000 visitors to the Oil Tank Culture Park, where distributor KOIL deployed LD Systems PA across separate zones. MAUI P900 columns, developed with Porsche Design Studio, carried audio for racing films in the motorsport gallery, MAUI 28 G3 systems covered the picnic garden, MAUI 11 G3 MIX units handled the kids' zone and six STINGER 10 G3 speakers supported the main stage. The P900 was first shown as a concept at Prolight + Sound in 2017.

## Dynacord's PowerMate 3000-X puts mixer and amp in 3U

Dynacord has introduced the PowerMate 3000-X, a 3U amplified digital mixer with eight mic/line inputs, two stereo effects processors and a four-channel, 3,000 W amplifier that can drive low- or high-impedance loads, including 100-volt lines. Control runs in a web browser on PCs, Macs and tablets with no software to install, with password-protected admin settings, GPIO and an automixer and ducker for speech and playback. It is aimed at worship spaces, schools, music and sports venues, as well as live events.
