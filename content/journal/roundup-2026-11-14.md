## Yamaha agrees to buy media server specialist Disguise

Yamaha Corporation has agreed to pay £215m for Butterfly Topco, the Carlyle-backed owner of Disguise, with completion expected in December. Yamaha says it plans to widen distribution of Disguise products in Japan, Asia and other regions and to develop products with closer audio and video integration. Disguise will keep its brand, and a combined roadmap is due in 2027. For touring and virtual production crews, the deal places one of the most widely used media server platforms inside a major audio manufacturer.

## Bose Professional introduces the MA3 Micro Array

Bose Professional has launched the MA3, a compact, passive and modular micro line array for fixed installation in reverberant, design-sensitive rooms. Each module pairs a 1-inch compression driver with four 2.25-inch woofers, and installers can adjust splay angles to shape vertical coverage, flying the array with a suspension bracket or fixing it to a wall. Bose pitches it as a "one-person install", which could bring steerable array coverage to smaller worship, hospitality and civic projects.

## Unilumin LED screens go up across Shenzhen for APEC 2026

Unilumin LED displays have been installed across Shenzhen ahead of the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting on 18–19 November. The media centre has 350 square metres of displays, including fine-pitch MIP screens, while large screens at the airport, Shenzhen North Railway Station, the metro and public plazas show APEC-themed content around the clock. It is a sizeable example of LED being used as city-wide event infrastructure rather than within a single venue.

## How Shure wireless handled the 2026 MTV VMAs

A newly published account of the MTV Video Music Awards at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles details a high-channel-count wireless set-up in a dense RF environment. Axient Digital carried the microphone channels and PSM 1000 handled in-ear monitoring, with ShowLink giving the RF team remote control of transmitters without interrupting performers or the broadcast. Soundtronics Wireless coordinated RF and antennas, with ATK as the main audio provider, a useful reference for anyone planning wireless on large televised shows.
