## An LED court makes its professional handball debut in Cologne

ASB GlassFloor's LED video sports floor, built on Absen's PL4.8W Lite V10 panels, was used at a professional handball match for the first time at the German Handball Cup in Cologne's Lanxess Arena. Almost 1,000 square metres of court carried player introductions, animated graphics reacting to key moments and sponsor content, replacing floor stickers and physical branding. The surface is accredited by the International Handball Federation and was installed and tested within 24 hours.

## NEXO in every cabin of a Polar Express ride in Wales

Total Sound Solutions has fitted out Brecon Mountain Railway's Polar Express experience with NEXO audio, using PS10 and LS600 speakers in the pre-ride marquee and at the North Pole stop. Each cabin has a pair of compact ID14s, an IDS108 sub and a nanoNXAMP4, whose onboard mixer takes two wireless mics and the QLab feed with no separate desk. The ID14s were chosen "due to their size and quality" for a train with no overhead space.

## INFiLED's TLens targets bright DOOH with far less power

INFiLED's TLens design gives the red, green and blue emitters in each LED their own reflector cups and micro-lenses rather than a shared cavity, reducing optical crosstalk and directing more light towards the viewer. In testing, an MV10.4mk2 display reached 10,000 nits at 255 W per square metre of LED power, against 650 W for an equivalent SMD display, and the package withstood higher thrust loads. It is aimed at roadside billboards and other high-brightness displays that run for long hours.

## ISE 2027 plans a 1,500-drone show and a smart workplace

ISE 2027, at Fira de Barcelona from 2 to 5 February, will grow its drone show from last year's 600 drones to 1,500, supplied by LANG AG, in a piece called "The Waking Cabinet" directed by Alice Labourel, with performances at 6pm on 2 to 4 February. The show will also stage a simulated "intelligent workplace" with Crestron, Legrand AV, Logitech, Microsoft, Q-SYS, Shure and Sony. Free tech tours return, alongside new paid tours of Barcelona Airport's control room and the Barça Immersive Tour.
