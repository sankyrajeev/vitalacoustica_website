## Martin Audio for the Chaturmas Bhakti Concert in Mumbai

VRS PRO supplied a Martin Audio system for the Chaturmas Bhakti Concert, an evening of devotional music with Rishabh and Sambhav Jain at the Police Hockey Ground in Mumbai. Twelve WPC elements per side formed the main hangs, with WPM centre fills, TORUS and Blackline out fills, LE200 monitors and 16 SXH218 subwoofers in a cardioid arrangement to control energy behind the stage. The engineer said the combination "gave us incredible control right across the venue".

[Read more at ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/martin-audio-delivers-at-chaturmas-bhakti-concert-in-india/)

## Tate Modern first in the UK with Christie's hybrid laser cinema projector

Tate Modern has replaced a lamp-based projector in its Starr Cinema with a Christie CP4415m-RGBH, a 4K hybrid RGB laser and phosphor model, the first in a UK venue. Installed by Omnex with a CineLife+ media block, it had to deliver high contrast and switch between DCP and other sources such as laptops, Blu-ray and live video in one sitting, helped by standard SDI inputs. Tate, which as a public body must meet project-value thresholds, says other quotes were around three times the cost.

## More than 1,000 Sennheiser products across the University of Manchester

The University of Manchester estimates it now has more than 1,000 Sennheiser products in use across a campus of over 220 buildings, with microphones in nearly every lecture theatre. It has moved through several generations of wireless to licence-free SpeechLine Digital Wireless, and uses TeamConnect Ceiling 2 microphones with PTZ cameras in larger rooms and TeamConnect Bars in smaller ones. The AV team reports that no TC Bar has failed or gone back for warranty repair.

## Next IPMX certification event set for Geneva in January

AIMS, the Video Services Forum and the EBU will hold the next IPMX product testing and certification event at EBU headquarters in Geneva from 18 to 22 January 2027, adding USB 2.0 transport to the test plan for KVM workflows. The first two events led to more than 80 products being certified in 2026, and member companies must submit products by 14 December. For integrators weighing open AV-over-IP, IPMX builds on ST 2110 transport and NMOS control, with HDCP handling and encryption still in progress.
