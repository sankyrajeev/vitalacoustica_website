## Candlebox carry their own powered monitor rig on tour

Seattle rock band Candlebox took a compact dBTechnologies VIO monitor system on their 32-date autumn tour: six W12T coaxial wedges and two X315 three-way loudspeakers as side fills, all with onboard Class-D amplification. Guitarist Peter Klett used two rear wedges as guitar fills in place of cabinets and amplifiers, while the side fills carried keyboards. The tour manager credits the powered boxes with saving truck space, stage space and time, and the set-up also reduced spill into vocal mics, giving the FOH engineer more control of the house mix.

## Jonathan Phillips becomes president of Renkus-Heinz

Renkus-Heinz has named Jonathan Phillips, previously its chief revenue officer, as president, with Monika Smetona taking the role of chief financial officer while remaining on the board. The changes took effect on 16 November after more than a year of joint preparation. Phillips speaks of "combining the best of a family business with industry best practices", and Ralph Heinz continues as chief technology officer.

## Producers and engineers among the CMA Awards winners

The 60th CMA Awards in Nashville included two categories that recognise producers and engineers, both won by Ella Langley's work. "Choosin' Texas" took single of the year and Dandelion took album of the year, produced by Langley with Miranda Lambert and Ben West (and Devin Dawson on the album), with both mixed by Dave Clauss. Langley also set a record for the most CMA wins in a single year, including entertainer of the year.
