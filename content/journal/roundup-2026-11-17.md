## Snow Patrol fit a whole in-ear show into one wideband channel

For three September shows at London's Royal Albert Hall, Snow Patrol ran Sennheiser Spectera for both in-ear monitoring and vocal handhelds. The set-up carried 14 stereo mixes to 24 bodypacks within a single 8 MHz carrier, with three antennas around the stage and an extra one for backstage crew, and IP remote access let the monitor engineer adjust settings from his mix position. As the monitor tech put it, they fit the "whole show into a single wideband channel", a useful data point for anyone planning in-ear systems in a crowded RF environment.

## 4Wall adds Elation KL Battens and more Paragon profiles

4Wall is rolling out Elation's KL Batten 72, 48 and 12 across its US locations, alongside a larger fleet of Paragon M and Paragon S LED profiles. The IP65 battens use a five-colour RGBMA engine with a CRI of 94.9, pixel control and colour correction from 2400K to 8500K, with the KL Batten 72 rated at 61,275 fixture lumens. Their arrival in a major US rental inventory makes the outdoor-rated washes easier to specify, with the first units slated for the University of Michigan's football season.

[Read more at PLSN](https://plsn.com/newsroom/product-news/4wall-adds-elation-kl-battens-expands-paragon-fleet/)

## Hexagon's MANTIS records set geometry as the camera rolls

Hexagon has introduced MANTIS by Leica Geosystems, a camera-mounted system that captures scene geometry and camera movement during filming. With MANTIS Studio, the on-set data becomes LiDAR-corrected depth maps, point clouds and camera tracks aligned with the footage, exported as EXR, FBX and Alembic. For virtual production and VFX teams, spatial data recorded alongside the picture could reduce the matching and tracking work that follows a shoot.

[Read more at TV Tech](https://www.tvtechnology.com/production/hexagon-introduces-camera-mounted-3d-capture-and-tracking-system)

## KV2 Audio tames a reverberant concert hall at York

The University of York's 350-seat Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall, a reverberant hexagonal room, has a new KV2 Audio system after earlier PAs struggled with uneven coverage and poor clarity. Two ESR106 loudspeakers hang beneath the lighting bars as main left and right, with an EX26 centre fill, two EX1.8 subwoofers on carts and an ESR2600D amplifier, chosen after a demonstration at PLASA Focus Leeds and on site. The university wanted to avoid large boxes or flown line arrays, and reports better detail and consistency across the hall, with some listeners not noticing the speakers at all.
