## Yamaha firmware brings bus GEQ and scene safes to MGX mixers

Yamaha has released firmware updates for its MGX mixers, URX audio interfaces and CC1 DAW controller. On MGX and URX, the FLEX12GEQ graphic EQ can now sit on stereo and mix output buses, a new Recall Safe option sets which channels and parameters a stored scene touches, and a Console Lock protects the interface. For engineers running monitor mixes or busy changeovers on smaller consoles, bus GEQ and recall safes are practical additions; CC1 users also gain plug-in profiles and wider MIDI support.

[Read more at Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/news/yamaha-launch-mgx-urx-cc1-updates)

## Danish rental house Profox adds Robe iEsprite LTL and Arianne6

Profox, a rental and production company near Aalborg in Denmark, has bought 52 Robe iEsprite LTL and 56 Arianne6 LED wash beams, the latter the first in the country. Its technical director describes IP-rated fixtures and easy maintenance as essential, and the service team reports the sealed units take about half the time to service of other moving lights in stock. The fixtures have already worked at Smukfest and on Tobias Rahim's summer tour, showing the weight outdoor and touring work puts on sealed, low-maintenance fixtures.

[Read more at PLSN](https://plsn.com/newsroom/all-news/profox-sets-a-pace-with-new-robe-investments/)

## NEXO GEO M10 gives a Washington church one sound for every seat

Evangelical Chinese Church in Washington state has replaced a piecemeal system with seven NEXO GEO M10 per side and a flown cardioid subwoofer array, designed in NS-1 and installed by Morgan Sound. The design aimed for even coverage without front fills or delays, and the upgrade added Dante networking, standardised routing, analogue failover and scene-based operation for a volunteer crew serving English and Mandarin services. As the volunteer technical lead put it, the biggest change was "having everyone hear the same thing", a useful benchmark for any room with a mixed and rotating team.
