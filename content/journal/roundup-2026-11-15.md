## d&b SL-Series system for Shakira's Madrid residency

Clair Global supplied a d&b audiotechnik system for Shakira's 13-show residency at a purpose-built stadium at Iberdrola Music in Madrid, which drew more than 700,000 visitors. GSL arrays covered the main and side hangs, with flown and ground-stacked SL-SUB, KSL delays and D90 amplifiers, and ArrayProcessing was used to even out coverage. With local sound limits and a hotel nearby, the system's directional control was used to keep energy on the audience and reduce spill off site, a familiar problem for anyone running large outdoor shows near homes.

## Void and Areal bring spatial audio to Hollywood's Vine Street

Void Acoustics has finished two projects outside and inside Avalon Hollywood: a permanent custom five-way system based on its Incubus design, and an eight-point spatial set-up for the SIDEPIECE Hollywood Block Party in early October. Both use Areal technology, which takes ordinary stereo material and spreads it across individual loudspeakers, so DJs do not need specially prepared immersive mixes. The block party's eight inward-facing towers were said to help limit spill into the surrounding neighbourhood.

## L-Acoustics replaces hidden ceiling speakers at a Texas parish

St. Ann Catholic Parish in Coppell, Texas, which averages about 10,000 worshippers a week, has replaced a ceiling system that lacked low-frequency response with an L-Acoustics design by Salas O'Brien, installed by Digital Resources. Syva columns with Syva Low subwoofers flank the altar, supported by X8i and X4i fill and delay positions and nine LA2Xi amplified controllers, with acoustic treatment added to control low-mid build-up. The colour-matched columns were chosen partly as the "least visually intrusive" option, a common priority in heritage and worship spaces.

## Vijit Chao Phraya lights up Bangkok's riverfront for a month

The Tourism Authority of Thailand has opened the fifth Vijit Chao Phraya festival, running from 13 November to 13 December across 19 riverfront locations, free to view from 18.00 to 22.00 each night. The programme combines light and sound shows, projection mapping, lasers, fireworks and drone formations, including 800-drone shows at Wat Arun on selected evenings. It is one of the region's larger public showcases for outdoor lighting, projection and drone work.
