## Upmixing stereo for immersive work at Future Phonic Studios

Richard Burki, founder of Future Phonic Studios in the Netherlands, has spent more than a decade on immersive and spatial audio for music, film, VR, theatre and installations. He uses NUGEN Audio's Halo Upmix to turn stereo material into immersive formats while keeping it compatible across playback systems, describing it as a bridge between "stereo sources and native 3D content". For teams building spatial shows or installations from existing stereo assets, it is a practical look at one working method.

[Read more at ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/nugen-audio-key-to-richard-burkis-immersive-audio-productions/)

## fedDSP's Kami compressor is free for its first two weeks

fedDSP has released Kami, a compressor plug-in with gentle pedal-style saturation, free for two weeks from its 18 November launch and $39 afterwards. The basic view has just two controls, while an advanced view adds pre and post filters, parallel mix, wet-path polarity and delay, and three limiter modes, with zero reported latency. It runs as VST3, AU and AAX on Windows and macOS.

[Read more at Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/news/feddsp-introduce-kami)

## LEA Professional amplifiers added to the Specifi platform

LEA Professional's Network Connect and Dante Connect amplifiers are now available in Specifi, a business management platform for AV integrators founded in Belfast in 2021. Integrators can drop the amplifiers straight into room-by-room proposals and pull product information into system designs. The amplifiers offer cloud-based monitoring and management, with the Dante Connect models also supporting Dante and AES67.

[Read more at ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/lea-professional-is-now-live-in-specifi-for-expanded-integrator-access/)
